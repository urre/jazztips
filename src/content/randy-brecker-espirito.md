Espírito is a 2026 album by American trumpeter Randy Brecker, released on the SoNo Recording Group (SRG) label. It is Brecker's first solo release featuring all-new original compositions in many years.

The project was composed, arranged, and produced by Architects Of Sound, the writing and production team of Claude Villani and Jeff Lorber. The album contains ten tracks: Ven Aquí, Pacific Blue, Sophia, Hot Sauce, Time Flies, Moonlight, Espírito, Butterscotch, In the Side Pocket, and Before We Say Goodbye, with a total running time of approximately 39 minutes.

Brecker is joined on the recording by Jeff Lorber on keyboards, Erick Walls on guitar, David Mann on saxophone, Gary Novak and John 'JR' Robinson on drums, Cornelius Mims on bass, and Lenny Castro on percussion. Espírito is available on CD, cobalt blue vinyl, and across digital and streaming platforms.
