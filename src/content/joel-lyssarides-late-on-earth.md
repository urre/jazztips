Late on Earth is the 2026 ACT Music release by Swedish pianist Joel Lyssarides, performing in his long-standing trio with Niklas Fernqvist on bass and Rasmus Blixt on drums. The album was recorded on 7–8 July 2025 at Bauer Studios in Ludwigsburg, Germany, with Adrian von Ripka handling recording, mixing, and mastering.

All twelve compositions on the album were written by Lyssarides, and the record was developed over a period of more than four years in close collaboration with producer Andreas Brandis. The track list includes Bortom Bergen, Mahabalipuram, Life in Between, In Itinere, Sotira, Never Alone, Raqs Sharqi, Folie À Deux, Intermission, Anemoia, Follow the Night, and the title track Late on Earth.

The album cover features artwork by Jiří Georg Dokoupil. Late on Earth is issued on CD, 180g black vinyl, limited Curaçao blue vinyl, and digital formats under the ACT catalogue number 8021.
