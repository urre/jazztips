Points of Light is the debut album by Ursa Major, bassist Christian McBride's quintet, released on Mack Avenue Records on October 30, 2026. McBride began putting the band together in 2022. He describes the album as an extension of what the group has played live over the previous four years.

The quintet is McBride on acoustic and electric bass, Nicole Glover on tenor and soprano saxophone, Ely Perlman on guitar, Mike King on piano and keyboards, and Savannah Harris on drums. The album was produced by Terrace Martin, who also plays synthesizer and vocoder, with additional synthesizers and keyboards from Quintin Gulledge.

Every member of the band contributed original compositions. The one cover is George Duke's "No Rhyme, No Reason", which closes the eight-track album.
