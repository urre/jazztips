Sing Seven Seas: Vol. 1 is a 2026 album by British composer, arranger, conductor, and trombonist Callum Au, released on Chairman Music in association with ECN Music. It is the first instalment of a two-volume project written for an expanded ensemble of more than 80 musicians and funded in part through a Kickstarter campaign.

All seven tracks on Volume 1 — Swipe Right!, Galt's Motor, Si Vis Pacem Para Carnyx, Tethys, Murmurätions, The Weaver, and jazz.ai — are composed, arranged, and conducted by Au. The first volume foregrounds his own writing and moves across a range of styles and textures, while the second volume is planned around the big-band tradition and his influences.

The band draws on a large pool of British and international jazz musicians, with featured soloists including Freddie Gavita on trumpet, Mark Nightingale on trombone, Emma Rawicz, Duncan Hemstock, Chad LB, and Nadim Teimoori on saxophones, alongside Au's own trombone playing. Au is also widely known as an arranger and orchestrator for artists such as Michael Bublé, RAYE, and Josh Groban.
