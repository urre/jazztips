New Orleans-born, New York City-based pianist Sullivan Fortner is an adept, award-winning jazz musician and composer with a bent toward sophisticated post-bop. Since 2015, Fortner has built an impressive list of credits playing with vibraphonist Stefon Harris, trumpeter Theo Croker, saxophonist Donald Harrison, and trumpeter Roy Hargrove. His album 'Moments Preserved' was released in 2018 on Impulse! Records. The album features Sullivan Fortner on piano, Ameen Saleem on double bass, Jeremy "Bean" Clemmons on drums, and Roy Hargrove on flugelhorn and trumpet on tracks 5 and 11.
