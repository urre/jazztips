Plays For Trane, Vol. 2 is an album by Swedish saxophonist Elin Forkelid, released on September 25, 2026 on Sail Cabin Records. It follows Elin Forkelid Plays For Trane from 2020 and uses the same five-piece band. The album was recorded by Niclas Lindström at Atlantis Metronome in Stockholm on June 9–10, 2025, and produced by Forkelid.

Forkelid plays tenor, alto and soprano saxophones. She is joined by David Stackenäs on guitar, Mattias Ståhl on vibraphone, Vilhelm Bromander on double bass and Jon Fält on drums.

All five pieces are John Coltrane compositions. The album opens with the roughly 16-minute medley "Dearly Beloved/Sun Star/Venus", followed by "Expression", "Naima", "Peace On Earth" and "Reverend King". Many of them come from Coltrane's later period. It was released digitally and as a limited CD and LP.
