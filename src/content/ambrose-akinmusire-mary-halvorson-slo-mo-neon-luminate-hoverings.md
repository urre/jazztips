Slo-Mo Neon Luminate Hoverings is the debut duo album by trumpeter Ambrose Akinmusire and guitarist Mary Halvorson, released by Nonesuch Records on 12 June 2026. The nine-track program sets Akinmusire's trumpet against Halvorson's guitar and comprises four compositions by each musician along with one co-written piece.

The two rehearsed the material in January 2025 and performed it at the New York club The Stone, recording the album the following day at Sear Sound. The record runs roughly 46 minutes.
