Leave That In There is the sixth album by New Orleans-born pianist, composer and bandleader Sullivan Fortner, released on 28 August 2026 via Artwork Records. It is the first studio recording by his working trio with bassist Tyrone Allen II and drummer Kayvon Gordon, and was co-produced by Derrick Hodge and Jack DeBoe.

The thirteen-track programme comprises twelve Fortner originals and one cover, among them 'Sugarcane', 'Que Diablos', 'Aerobatics Pt. 1' and 'Pt. 2', 'Sorcerer's Stick', 'Number 3', 'Aria II' and 'A Hymn for Linda'. The music moves between acoustic trio playing, Hammond B3 organ, synthesizers, marimba, Bata drumming and fully improvised passages, extending the group beyond a conventional piano trio format.

The title comes from Spike Lee's 2006 documentary When the Levees Broke: A Requiem in Four Acts, about the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina. Fortner has described the record as drawing on family history, spiritual traditions, New Orleans history and callbacks to earlier work in his own catalogue.
