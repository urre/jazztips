Johnny Griffin Sextet is a studio album by tenor saxophonist Johnny Griffin, recorded on February 25, 1958 in New York City and released on the Riverside label (RLP 12-264) later that year. It was Griffin's first album as leader for Riverside, produced by Orrin Keepnews, and was recorded the day before the sessions for his follow-up Way Out!.

The date assembles an all-star three-horn front line, with Griffin joined by trumpeter Donald Byrd and baritone saxophonist Pepper Adams, backed by a rhythm section of Kenny Drew on piano, Wilbur Ware on bass and Philly Joe Jones on drums.

The program comprises five tracks: the originals "Stix' Trix", "Johnny G.G." and "Catharsis" alongside the standards "What's New?" and Dizzy Gillespie's "Woody 'n' You". The original cover design was by Paul Bacon, with photography by Lawrence N. Shustak.
