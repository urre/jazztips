What Day Is It is the debut LP from Fonville x Fribush, released February 27, 2026 via WarHen Records. The 10-track album was cut live over two days at Minimum Wage Recording in Richmond, Virginia, part of a session that yielded more than 30 takes in total.

The trio is drummer Corey Fonville (Butcher Brown), organist Sam Fribush, and guitarist Alan Good Parker, who also contributes pedal steel. Sam Fribush's 1962 Hammond B3 organ anchored the sessions; the studio's owner reportedly had to remove a door to fit the instrument into the tracking room.

The record was produced by Corey Fonville and Sam Fribush, recorded and mixed by Lance Koehler at Minimum Wage Recording, and mastered by Rob Dobson at Hear Garden in Los Angeles. WarHen Records issued the album on Blue Chips vinyl (limited to 125 copies) and Classic Black vinyl (limited to 200 copies), alongside a digital release.
