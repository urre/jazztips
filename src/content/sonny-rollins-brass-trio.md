Brass/Trio is a 1958 album by American tenor saxophonist Sonny Rollins, originally released on the MetroJazz label and later reissued on Verve. The record pairs two contrasting settings on a single LP: a brass-ensemble session on one side and a saxophone trio session on the other.

The brass tracks (Far Out East, Who Cares?, Love Is a Simple Thing, and Grand Street) were recorded in New York on 11 July 1958 with a large ensemble arranged and conducted by Ernie Wilkins. The personnel includes Nat Adderley on cornet, Clark Terry, Reunald Jones, and Ernie Royal on trumpets, Billy Byers, Jimmy Cleveland, and Frank Rehak on trombones, Don Butterfield on tuba, Dick Katz on piano, René Thomas on guitar, Henry Grimes on bass, and Roy Haynes on drums.

The trio tracks (Manhattan, If You Were the Only Girl in the World, and Body and Soul) were recorded the day before, on 10 July 1958, with Rollins joined only by Henry Grimes on bass and Specs Wright on drums. The two sessions place Rollins in deliberately different contexts on the same release, contrasting orchestrated arrangements with a stripped-down rhythm-section setting.
