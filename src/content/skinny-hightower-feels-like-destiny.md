Feels Like Destiny is the seventh album by American multi-instrumentalist, composer and producer Skinny Hightower, released on August 21, 2026 by CastOver Records. It contains ten tracks with a total running time of approximately 56 minutes.

Hightower, who is from Wichita, Kansas, wrote and recorded the album over a period of about 24 weeks and performs nearly every instrument himself, including acoustic piano, Fender Rhodes, Wurlitzer, Clavinet, Hammond B3 organ, vibraphone, synthesizers, guitar, bass, Moog bass, drums and percussion. He also handled the string and horn arrangements and the production. The only additional performers are flautists Althea Rene, who appears on the opening track "Devotion", and Charlotte Glasson.

The music draws on contemporary smooth jazz, neo-soul and instrumental R&B, with 1970s soul and funk as recurring reference points, and is centred on Hightower's acoustic piano playing. The track listing is "Devotion", "Chain Reaction", "Between the Lines", "Nimbus", "Velour", "The Fifth Floor", "Strides", "Hypnotic", "Ridin' High" and "Deeper Than You Think".

The album follows Hightower's 2016 debut Cloud Nine and later releases including Retrospect, Mind Over Matter and Analog Theory. It was issued in a Hi-Res edition on streaming and download platforms.
