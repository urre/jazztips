Universal Truth is pianist Emmet Cohen's fourth album for Mack Avenue Records, released on May 29, 2026. It was recorded in the centennial year of Miles Davis and John Coltrane, and its programme is built around material and ideas associated with both figures.

The eight-track sequence pairs standards and jazz repertoire with new writing: Budo, Thelonious Monk's Well You Needn't, My Funny Valentine, Jimmy Heath's Gingerbread Boy and Coltrane's Blue Train sit alongside Cohen's three-part title suite, comprising I. Eternal Glimpse, II. Compassion and III. Universal Truth. The suite takes its name from a phrase Cohen encountered in his research on Coltrane. Total running time is roughly 42 minutes.

Cohen's working trio with bassist Yasushi Nakamura and drummer Joe Farnsworth forms the core group, expanded by trumpeter Jeremy Pelt and tenor saxophonist Tivon Pennicott. Bassist Ron Carter appears on Well You Needn't, My Funny Valentine and Gingerbread Boy, and tenor saxophonist George Coleman on My Funny Valentine and Blue Train, the closing track bringing both tenors together with Pelt and the trio. Cohen produced the album with Christian Wiggs.
