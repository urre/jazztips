Space Bomb is a 2026 album credited to Charlie Hunter, Corey Fonville, Andrew Randazzo, and DJ Harrison, released on the SideHustle label. It pairs the American seven-string guitarist Charlie Hunter — who handles both guitar and bass lines on his signature hybrid instrument — with members of the Richmond, Virginia ensemble Butcher Brown: drummer Corey Fonville, bassist Andrew Randazzo, and keyboardist/producer DJ Harrison.

The record contains 15 tracks and runs roughly 40 minutes, moving between acid jazz, funk, soul-jazz, and instrumental hip-hop-leaning grooves. Guest appearances include drummer Stanton Moore on The Breaks, and guitarist Will Bernard and keyboardist Robert Walter on Emphasis! (On Parenthesis).

The album follows a long line of Charlie Hunter releases exploring trio and small-group instrumental music, and is one of several recent collaborations between Hunter and players associated with the Butcher Brown circle.
