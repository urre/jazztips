Hi Voltage is a studio album by tenor saxophonist Hank Mobley, released on Blue Note Records (BST 84273) in March 1968. It was recorded on October 9, 1967 at Van Gelder Studio in Englewood Cliffs, New Jersey, and produced by Francis Wolff.

Mobley leads a sextet with three horns up front: Jackie McLean on alto saxophone and Blue Mitchell on trumpet. The rhythm section is pianist John Hicks, bassist Bob Cranshaw and drummer Billy Higgins.

Mobley wrote all six pieces: "High Voltage", "Two and One", "No More Goodbyes", "Advance Notice", "Bossa De Luxe" and "Flirty Gerty". The album was reissued on CD in 2004 in Blue Note's Rudy Van Gelder Edition series.
