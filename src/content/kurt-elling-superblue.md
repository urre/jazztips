SuperBlue is an album by the American jazz vocalist Kurt Elling, released on 8 October 2021 by Edition Records. It was initiated by guitarist Charlie Hunter, who contacted Elling during the pandemic about a project built on funk and groove, and who produced the record. The band is completed by keyboardist and bassist DJ Harrison and drummer Corey Fonville, both members of the Richmond group Butcher Brown.

The ten-track programme was recorded live in the studio by engineer Adrian Olson at Montrose Recording in Richmond, Virginia. It combines new material with reworkings of compositions by Wayne Shorter and Freddie Hubbard and a stripped-down reading of Cody ChesnuTT's 'The Seed'. Tracks include 'SuperBlue', 'Sassy', 'Manic Panic Epiphanic', 'Where to Find It', 'Dharma Bums' and 'Endless Lawns'.

The album marked a departure from Elling's earlier acoustic post-bop and vocalese work, placing his voice against synth-funk and hip-hop-informed rhythms. It received a Grammy nomination for Best Jazz Vocal Album at the 2022 ceremony, and spawned a series of follow-ups under the SuperBlue name, including The London Sessions (2022) and The Iridescent Spree (2023). The catalogue is now issued through Elling's Big Shoulders Records.
