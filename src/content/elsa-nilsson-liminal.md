Liminal is a 2026 album by the Swedish-born, Brooklyn-based flutist and composer Elsa Nilsson, released on Adhyâropa Records on 12 June 2026 and credited to her group Band of Pulses. The quartet pairs Nilsson's flutes and electronics with pianist Santiago Leibson, bassist Marty Kenney, and drummer Rodrigo Recabarren.

The album's eight pieces are built around the idea of liminal, in-between states and the process of making and acting on major life decisions. Track titles include Andetag, Capacity, Mourning for Two, Transition State, and 1 year, 10 months, 3 days for Ahmaud Arbery. The recording was mixed and mastered by Sam Minaie.
