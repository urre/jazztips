Tomorrow We'll Figure Out the Rest is a 2025 album by Norwegian jazz vocalist Silje Nergaard, released on Masterworks via Sony Music. The album is dedicated to Nergaard's parents and family, and weaves together newly written songs with brief spoken-word interludes drawn from family recordings.

The core band includes Helge Lien on piano, Jarle Vespestad on drums, Finn Guttormsen on bass, George 'Jojje' Wadenius on guitar, and Håkon Kornstad on saxophone, with Martin Windstad on percussion. The Stavanger Symphony Orchestra appears on several tracks under the direction of Vince Mendoza, who also wrote the orchestral arrangements.

The record contains thirteen tracks, including You Are the Very Moon, Lover Man, A Perfect Night to Fall in Love, Before You Happened to Me, Dance Me Love, My Man My Man, Brooklyn Rain, a reading of Here There and Everywhere, and the title track Tomorrow We'll Figure Out the Rest.
