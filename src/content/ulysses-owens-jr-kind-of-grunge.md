Kind of Grunge is an album by drummer Ulysses Owens Jr. and his band Generation Y, released on August 14, 2026 by Cellar Live in partnership with Musora Media. It was recorded live in one-take sessions at Musora Studios on April 20–22, 2026, and produced by Ulysses Owens Jr. and Ron Jackson.

The project grew out of Owens' appearances in Drumeo's "For the First Time" series, in which he reinterpreted Nirvana songs without having heard the originals. After the performances drew a large online audience, Musora founder Jared Falk encouraged the ensemble to record a full-length album capturing the same spontaneous approach.

The twelve tracks translate songs from the grunge era into a modern jazz vocabulary, including Nirvana's "Smells Like Teen Spirit", "In Bloom" and "Heart-Shaped Box", Pearl Jam's "Even Flow", Alice in Chains' "Would?", Soundgarden's "Spoonman", Stone Temple Pilots' "Interstate Love Song", Mudhoney's "Touch Me I'm Sick", The Smashing Pumpkins' "Disarm", Temple of the Dog's "Wooden Jesus", Hole's "Celebrity Skin" and "Where Did You Sleep Last Night". The band features Benny Benack III on trumpet and vocals, Langston Hughes II on alto saxophone, Tyler Bullock on piano, Dan Wilson on guitar and Thomas Milovac on bass.
