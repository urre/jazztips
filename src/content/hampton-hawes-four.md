Four! is a studio album by pianist Hampton Hawes, recorded on January 27, 1958 and released on Contemporary Records. It was produced by Lester Koenig. The original cover bills the album as "Four! Hampton Hawes!!!! with Barney Kessel, Shelly Manne & Red Mitchell".

Hawes leads a quartet with guitarist Barney Kessel, bassist Red Mitchell and drummer Shelly Manne. The seven tracks combine standards, a Charlie Parker tune, a Mitchell original and a Hawes original: "Yardbird Suite", "There Will Never Be Another You", "Bow Jest", "Sweet Sue, Just You", "Up Blues", "Like Someone in Love" and "Love Is Just Around the Corner".

Original Jazz Classics reissued the album in 1991 with two bonus tracks, "Thou Swell" and "The Awful Truth". In 2022–2023 Craft Recordings issued a remastered edition, available as a 24-bit/96 kHz digital release and as part of its Contemporary Records 70th anniversary series.
