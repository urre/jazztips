Beyond The Missouri Sky (Short Stories) is a jazz album released in 1997 by the American musicians Pat Metheny and Charlie Haden. It was released on Verve Records. The album features a collection of jazz compositions, some written by Metheny and Haden, as well as interpretations of other works.

The album received critical acclaim, winning the Grammy Award for Best Jazz Instrumental Performance. It is noted for its acoustic and contemplative style, showcasing the duo's intricate musical interplay.
