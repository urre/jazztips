the munkedal tapes is the third album by the piano trio of Fabian Kallerdahl, Albin Lindgård and Ossian Ward, released on Hoob Records (HOOBLP135) on October 30, 2026. It follows the live albums Live at Unity (2021) and Live at Unity, Vol. 2 (2024), and is the trio's first studio recording.

The album was recorded over three spring days in a house near the Swedish west coast, with engineer Åke Linton. Kallerdahl plays piano, Lindgård double bass and Ward drums.

The eight tracks run 34 minutes and mix the trio's own pieces with interpretations of Chopin and Wagner, Chopin's Prelude No. 20 and Wagner's Tannhäuser Overture. "säsong", "tannhäuser overture" and "lugn" came out as singles before the album, which was released digitally and on LP.
