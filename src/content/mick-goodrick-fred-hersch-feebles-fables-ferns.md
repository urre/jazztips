Feebles, Fables & Ferns is a duo album by guitarist Mick Goodrick and pianist Fred Hersch, issued by ECM Records on 19 June 2026. Although released in 2026, the music dates from August 1988, when the two recorded together at Hersch's studio in New York; the tapes went unreleased for nearly four decades before ECM drew them from its archive.

The session documents a one-time meeting between the musicians, who were both active around Boston at the time. Goodrick, who died in 2022, was a guitarist and longtime Berklee teacher who had earlier recorded the ECM leader date In Pas(s)ing, while Hersch was then near the start of his career as a trio leader and has since become one of the most widely recognized pianists in jazz.

The program pairs original writing with standards and jazz compositions, including Falling Grace, Out of Nowhere, Heartsong, and Soul Eyes.
