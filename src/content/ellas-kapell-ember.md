Ellas Kapell's fourth album, 'Ember,' is a bold step into modern vocal jazz. Known for their emotionally charged interpretations of standards, the Swedish quartet expands their sonic landscape. The album is self-produced, showcasing a new level of artistic control and blending acoustic and electronic elements.

Released on February 27th, 2026, via Naxos Prophone, 'Ember' reimagines jazz standards such as 'Very Early' and 'All the Things You Are.' The group, featuring vocalist Lovisa Jennervall, Manne Skafvenstedt (piano, synths), August Eriksson (double and electric bass, violin), and Edvin Glänte (drums, percussion), delivers intimate yet expansive music. Additional instrumentation includes Johan Christoffersson (alto sax), Tobias Wiklund (cornet), and Gustav Alte (pedal steel). The album balances jazz tradition with fresh creative heat, inviting new audiences beyond the traditional jazz sphere.
