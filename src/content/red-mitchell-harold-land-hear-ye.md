Hear Ye! is the only album by the Red Mitchell–Harold Land Quintet, co-led by bassist Red Mitchell and tenor saxophonist Harold Land. It was recorded on October 14 and December 13, 1961 and released on Atlantic Records (LP 1376); some sources give the release year as 1962.

The quintet is Land on tenor saxophone, Mitchell on bass, Carmell Jones on trumpet, Frank Strazzeri on piano and Leon Petties on drums.

The six pieces are originals by the two leaders, among them Land's "Triplin' Awhile" and "Catacomb" and Mitchell's "Rosie's Spirit" and the title track. The album was later reissued on CD by Atlantic and Rhino.
