Osmosis is an album by Zohar & Adam, the New York duo of brothers Zohar Cabo and Adam Cabo, released on Mack Avenue Records on November 6, 2026. Zohar plays keyboards (mainly a Nord Stage 3, piano on two tracks) and Adam plays drums and percussion. The brothers also produced and engineered the record.

The brothers grew up playing in a family band with their sister and mother, and began improvising together as a duo during the pandemic. Their music draws on jazz, hip-hop, neo-soul and electronica. They have opened for Norah Jones, Gregory Porter and Christian McBride.

The ten tracks include guest appearances by bassist Christian McBride and saxophonist Steve Wilson on "For Chick", and by bassist Hadrien Feraud and Skylar Tang on the title track. Nory & J3PO appear on "Demon", Noga Cabo on "The Table" and Ben Mizrach on "Just One of Us".
