Without Even A Thread Of Clothing is a 2026 duo album by Swedish pianist and composer Lars Jansson and Danish bassist Jesper Bodilsen, released on the Mingus Records label. The recording pairs Jansson's piano with Bodilsen's double bass across twelve tracks, running roughly 56 minutes.

Both musicians are long-established figures in Scandinavian jazz. Jansson has a substantial catalogue of melodic, lyrical recordings as a leader and composer, while Bodilsen is a widely recorded bassist on the Danish and wider European scene. The album is set in a spare piano-and-bass format, with material that is largely melodic and introspective.

The program includes the title track alongside The Way Things Are, Loving Kindness, The Wounded Healer Can Heal, Hidden Beauty, Why Does Rain Fall?, A New Path, Marionette, Fauré in November, Little Song, and Resting in the Shadow. The recording was engineered by Thomas Vang and mixed and mastered by August Wanngren.
