Henriette Eilertsen Trio's debut album 'Moder' is characterized by captivating compositions and balanced interaction, creating a soundscape that is intimate and expansive. The leader's unmistakable tone and improvisational style are central, supported by Joel Ring on cello and Øystein Aarnes Vik on drums, who provide a light-footed and tight foundation of color and texture. The music, acoustically anchored and refined with abstract electronic gestures, features catchy tunes, chamber musical landscapes, and open passages of collective exploratory curiosity. Pianist Jon Balke contributes to three tracks, expanding the music with his non-compromising and timeless tone. The album navigates the flute's vast lineage, from rustic tool to refined instrument, blending ancient resonance with contemporary abstraction. The title 'Moder' (Mother) reflects a caring force that delves into both an earthly connection and a more abstract, overarching theme.
