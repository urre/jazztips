Armenian piano virtuoso Tigran Hamasyan's latest album Manifeste is a powerful fusion of jazz, progressive rock, and Armenian folk. Recorded across five countries between 2023 and 2025, the album blends rhythmic complexity, cinematic depth, and spiritual intensity, featuring longtime collaborators and the Yerevan State Chamber Choir. Manifeste explores themes of seeking, transformation, and creative renewal, unfolding as a ritual-like sequence from meditative pieces to powerful anthems. Hamasyan integrates Armenian folk and sacred sources with jazz improvisation, rock-derived rhythmic language, and compositions for solo piano, ensemble, and choir. The album is described as a bold declaration where the human, digital, and sacred unite in sound, showcasing Hamasyan's fascination with transcendence and transformation.
