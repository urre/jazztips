Alive With Ghosts Today is a 2026 album by American saxophonist Chris Potter, released on Edition Records. The recording is conceived as a suite inspired by the abolitionist John Brown and his 1859 raid on Harpers Ferry, tracing that history across eight pieces.

The album was recorded at Sear Sound in New York City on 24–26 March 2025. Potter leads an ensemble featuring Bill Frisell on guitar, Burniss Travis on electric bass, Nate Smith on drums, Rane Moore on clarinet, Zekkereya El-Magharbel on trombone, and Sara Caswell on violin. Potter plays tenor and soprano saxophone, and Frisell's guitar serves as a central voice within the arrangements.

All music is composed by Chris Potter, with the exception of John Coltrane's arrangement of the traditional Song of the Underground Railroad and an excerpt from the Largo (Going Home) of Antonín Dvořák's Symphony No. 9. The album was produced by Chris Potter, with Dave Stapleton and Louise Holland as executive producers, recorded by Chris Allen, mixed at Second Take Sound Studio B, and mastered by Nate Wood.
