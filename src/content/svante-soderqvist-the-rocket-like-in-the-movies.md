Swedish bassist and composer Svante Soderqvist returns with THE ROCKET to the newest chapter with their latest album Like In the Movies, out on March 13, 2026. Like In the Movies is a cinematic journey carved from the sounds of our time: searching, courageous, and profoundly human.On this album, listeners are taken deeper into Soderqvist's world of musical storytelling, one that resonates with the anxieties, tensions, and uncertainties of today's global reality. The compositions reflect a sense of unease about the challenges facing humanity, yet they also carry an undercurrent of hope. Shaped in close collaboration with the band members, equally internationally respected musicians Adam Forkelid, Calle Rasmusson, and Tuulikki Bartosik, the arrangements reveal a shared musical vision that adds depth and vibrancy to the narrative. In these pieces, music becomes a form of resilience, a reminder that creativity and imagination still hold power, even against all odds.
