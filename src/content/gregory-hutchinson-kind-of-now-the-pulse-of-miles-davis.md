Released on Warner Music on April 3, 2026, Kind of Now - The Pulse of Miles Davis is drummer and composer Gregory Hutchinson's tribute project marking the centennial of Miles Davis. Rather than recreating Davis's recordings, the album extends the conversation Davis began, with Hutchinson framing the project as a continuation of that lineage.

The ensemble pairs Hutchinson on drums with trumpeter Ambrose Akinmusire, tenor saxophonist and bass clarinetist Ron Blake, pianist Gerald Clayton, guitarists Jakob Bro and Emmanuel Michael, and bassist Joe Sanders. The program spans compositions by Miles Davis, Wayne Shorter, Tony Williams, and three Hutchinson originals, drawn from different periods of Davis's discography.

Hutchinson, who also produced the record, uses distinct tonal colors and cymbal choices as compositional tools across the session. The album incorporates trio settings, fusion-leaning passages, and three solo drum tracks, totaling roughly 57 minutes of music.
