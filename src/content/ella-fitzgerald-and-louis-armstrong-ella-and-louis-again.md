Ella and Louis Again is a studio album by Ella Fitzgerald and Louis Armstrong, released in October 1957 on Verve Records. It serves as the sequel to their 1956 collaboration, Ella and Louis. This album deviates from its predecessor by featuring seven solo vocal tracks by either Armstrong or Fitzgerald alongside its twelve duet tracks. The album was recorded at Radio Recorders and Capitol Studios in Hollywood. It was later reissued as part of a two-compact disc set in 1995 and included in The Complete Ella Fitzgerald & Louis Armstrong on Verve in 1997.
