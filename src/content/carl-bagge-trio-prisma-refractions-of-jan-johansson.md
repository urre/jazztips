Prisma - Refractions Of Jan Johansson is a 2026 album by the Swedish piano trio led by Carl Bagge, released on 13 November 2026 on the Prisma Music label. The album is devoted to the music associated with Jan Johansson, the Swedish pianist whose 1960s recordings of Swedish folk melodies, among them Jazz på svenska, became central to Swedish jazz.

The twelve tracks run about 47 minutes and include Swedish folk and traditional songs such as Kväsarvalsen, Stolt Karin getepiga, Bara en flicka, Klara stjärnor, Kung Erik leker på luta and Det vore synd att dö än. The title refers to Prisma, a Jan Johansson composition from his Innertrio recordings.

Carl Bagge is a Stockholm-based jazz pianist whose trio has released several albums, including the Långholmen Sessions series.
