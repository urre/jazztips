Sullivan Fortner's critically acclaimed album, "Moments Preserved," was released in 2018. The album showcases Fortner's deep-rooted talents as a pianist, composer, and bandleader, blending distinct elements from different musical eras while preserving tradition and evolving the sound. It primarily features a trio with bassist Ameen Saleem and drummer Jeremy "Bean" Clemons, with trumpeter and flugelhorn player Roy Hargrove contributing to three tracks. Fortner's artistry connects various musical styles, presenting compositions that are both deeply soulful and inventive. The album has been praised for its high-class, imaginative musicianship and dynamic mood changes, with some tracks featuring classical influences reminiscent of Sergei Rachmaninov.
