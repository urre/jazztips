Trilogy 3 is a live album by Chick Corea and his trio, featuring Corea on piano with Christian McBride on double bass and Brian Blade on drums. This three-disc album brings together live performances from their February-March 2020 tour, which was cut short by the onset of the pandemic. The album showcases their unparalleled chemistry and artistic brilliance, featuring fresh interpretations of jazz standards and Chick Corea originals, including the first trio recording of Corea's iconic composition 'Windows' since its appearance on the album Now He Sings, Now He Sobs, nearly six decades ago. It marks another milestone for this celebrated ensemble, following the critically acclaimed Trilogy (2013) and Trilogy 2 (2018) albums, both of which earned Grammy Awards.
