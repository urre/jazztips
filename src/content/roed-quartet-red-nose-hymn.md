Red Nose Hymn is the debut album by the Norwegian-Swedish modern jazz ensemble Røed Quartet, released on 22 May 2026 on the Sauajazz label (catalog SAU009).

The group is led by Norwegian saxophonist Aksel Røed and features his brother Lyder Røed on trumpet and flugelhorn, Norwegian bassist Ola Høyer on double bass, and Swedish drummer Cornelia Nilsson. The material on the album originated from a commissioned work the quartet performed at the Tedansfestival in 2024 and combines acoustic jazz with elements of psychedelic rock, ambient, and electronic textures.

The record contains five tracks: One Half Step for Me (A Whole Step for My Brother), Fanfare for Judee, Red Nose Hymn, Between the Wish and the World, and Time to Go. The album was recorded by Vetle Junker and mixed and mastered by Arne Martin Nybo, and is available on vinyl LP, CD, and digital formats.
