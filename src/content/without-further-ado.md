Christian McBride returns with "Without Further Ado, Vol. 1," a dynamic new release featuring his acclaimed big band. Released in 2025 on Mack Avenue Records, this album showcases McBride's extraordinary talents as a bassist, composer, arranger, and bandleader.

The album combines McBride's original compositions with fresh arrangements of selected standards, all delivered with the explosive energy and precise execution that has become the hallmark of the Christian McBride Big Band. Since winning multiple Grammy Awards for his previous big band efforts, McBride has continued to refine his approach to large ensemble writing, balancing sophisticated harmonic concepts with the driving swing and soulful grooves that are central to his musical identity.

"Without Further Ado" represents the first volume in what promises to be an exciting new series, allowing McBride to explore the expansive possibilities of the big band format. The recording features an all-star lineup of musicians who navigate the challenging charts with remarkable cohesion and individual brilliance, anchored throughout by McBride's powerful, virtuosic bass playing.
