West Coast Blues! is a studio album by tenor saxophonist Harold Land, released in 1960 on Jazzland Records. It was recorded at Fugazi Hall in San Francisco on May 17 and 18, 1960.

Land leads a sextet with guitarist Wes Montgomery, trumpeter Joe Gordon, pianist Barry Harris, bassist Sam Jones and drummer Louis Hayes. The cover credits Harris, Jones and Hayes as "the all-soul rhythm section".

The program includes three Land originals ("Ursula", "Terrain" and "Compulsion"), Charlie Parker's "Klactoveedsedstene", the ballad "Don't Explain" associated with Billie Holiday, and an early recording of Montgomery's composition "West Coast Blues".
