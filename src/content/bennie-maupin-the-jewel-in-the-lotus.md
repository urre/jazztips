The Jewel in the Lotus is the debut album by jazz woodwind player Bennie Maupin, recorded in March 1974 and released on ECM Records. It features a sextet with Herbie Hancock on acoustic and electric pianos, Buster Williams on bass, Billy Hart and Freddie Waits on drums, and Bill Summers on percussion. Charles Sullivan makes guest appearances on trumpet. The album's title is a translation of the Buddhist mantra "Om Mani Padme Hum". The music is described as deep, meditative, and spiritual, with influences from jazz-fusion, avant-garde jazz, and modal jazz. The album has been widely acclaimed as a jazz classic, noted for its rich soundscapes and the interplay of the musicians.
