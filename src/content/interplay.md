Interplay is a 1963 studio album by American jazz pianist Bill Evans. Recorded in July 1962 in New York City, the album was originally released by Riverside Records. It features a quintet lineup, with Evans on piano, Freddie Hubbard on trumpet, Jim Hall on guitar, Percy Heath on bass, and Philly Joe Jones on drums. This recording is notable for showcasing Evans in a quintet setting, departing from his more common trio format, and it helped establish him as a formidable bandleader. The album comprises a selection of jazz standards alongside the Bill Evans original title track. The recording sessions were produced by Orrin Keepnews.
