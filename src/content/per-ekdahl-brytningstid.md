Brytningstid is an album by Swedish drummer and composer Per Ekdahl, released April 10, 2026 on TengTones. The 13-track program runs roughly 49 minutes and includes pieces such as "Awakenings," "Krabbgång," "Waltz For Walt," the title track "Brytningstid," and "Stockholm i nordiskt ljus."

The Per Ekdahl Ensemble brings together a chamber-jazz lineup featuring Lisa Bodélius on trombone, Per Texas Johansson on woodwinds, Malin My Wall on violin, Erik Tengholm on trumpet, Gustaf Ljunggren on guitar and lap steel, Martin Höper on bass, and Carl Bagge on piano, with Ekdahl on drums. Many of the track titles are in Swedish, and the repertoire draws on threads from Swedish music history.

TengTones is a Swedish jazz label founded in 2021 by trumpeter Erik Tengholm and his brother Johan Tengholm.
