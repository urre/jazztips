"New Standards, Vol. 1" is a bold reimagining of what constitutes the jazz canon, led by master drummer, vocalist, and producer Terri Lyne Carrington. Released in 2023 on Candid Records, this album challenges traditional notions of the "Great American Songbook" by presenting contemporary compositions as worthy successors to the classic standards.

Carrington, a three-time Grammy winner and the founder of the Berklee Institute of Jazz and Gender Justice, brings together an exceptional ensemble of musicians for this project. The band features pianist Kris Davis, whose angular harmonic approach provides fresh perspectives on the material; bassist Linda May Han Oh, whose compositional voice and supportive yet adventurous playing anchor the group; trumpeter Nicholas Payton, adding his distinctive voice and genre-defying approach; and guitarist Matthew Stevens, whose contemporary sound palette expands the ensemble's sonic possibilities.

The album's repertoire includes works by contemporary composers and artists across various genres, treating songs by Esperanza Spalding, Robert Glasper, and others with the same reverence traditionally reserved for Gershwin and Porter. This conceptual framework suggests that today's generation deserves their own standards—songs that speak to contemporary experiences and sensibilities while maintaining the artistic depth and emotional resonance of the classics.

Carrington's drumming throughout is a masterclass in supportive yet dynamic playing, propelling the music forward while leaving ample space for her bandmates to express themselves. Her occasional vocals add an intimate dimension to certain tracks, demonstrating her multifaceted artistry beyond the drum kit.

"New Standards, Vol. 1" is more than just an album—it's a statement about the evolution of jazz repertoire and a celebration of the rich compositional voices of the 21st century. By positioning contemporary works as new standards, Carrington opens up conversations about canonization, tradition, and the future of the music, all while delivering performances of remarkable beauty and sophistication.
