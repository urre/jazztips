Another Time: The Hilversum Concert is a never-before-issued live recording by the Bill Evans Trio, featuring pianist Bill Evans, bassist Eddie Gomez, and drummer Jack DeJohnette. It was recorded at the Netherlands Radio Union (NRU) VARA Studio 8 in Hilversum, Holland, on June 22, 1968, and officially released by Resonance Records on September 1, 2017. This album is notable for being only the third known recording of this specific trio, which was active for a six-month period in 1968. The recording is praised for its exceptional sound quality, captured using the then state-of-the-art equipment of the NRU, and preserved in pristine condition over the years. This release followed the success of 'Some Other Time: The Lost Session from the Black Forest', which was recorded just two days prior to 'The Hilversum Concert'.
