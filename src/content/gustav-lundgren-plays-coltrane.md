Plays Coltrane is an album by Swedish guitarist Gustav Lundgren, released on September 25, 2026 on Prisma Music. It is a tribute to John Coltrane and came out in the year of Coltrane's centenary.

Lundgren is joined by pianist Rasmus Sørensen, bassist Pär-Ola Landin and drummer Jorge Rossy. The seven tracks are Coltrane compositions and pieces associated with him: "Moment's Notice", "Blues to You", "The Inch Worm", "Central Park West", "Mr. P.C.", "Like Sonny" and "Slowtrane".

The album follows Echoes of Coltrane, a five-track release by the same group from May 2026. Several pieces appear on both albums.
