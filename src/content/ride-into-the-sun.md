Brad Mehldau's "Ride into the Sun" showcases the pianist's continued evolution as one of contemporary jazz's most influential voices. Released in 2025 on Nonesuch Records, this album features Mehldau's long-standing trio with Larry Grenadier on bass and Jeff Ballard on drums.

The album combines Mehldau's original compositions with his distinctive interpretations of pieces from diverse sources, including the Velvet Underground song that gives the album its title. As with much of his work, Mehldau demonstrates his ability to bridge the worlds of jazz, classical, and popular music through his sophisticated harmonic approach and improvisational fluidity.

Mehldau's delicate touch and dynamic sensitivity are on full display throughout the recording, showcasing why he remains one of the most respected pianists in modern jazz. The trio's remarkable interplay—developed over decades of collaboration—creates a conversational approach to improvisation that feels both intimate and expansive.
